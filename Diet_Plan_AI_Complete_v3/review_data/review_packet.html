NutriCare: fictional cases for clinical review

Every case is synthetic, every label is blank, and every plan is unverified. These examples are for discussing dataset quality and suitability. They are not prescriptions. Compare candidate plans within a case, identify missing history or food/portion data, and leave the suitability blank if there is insufficient information. A review does not establish clinical efficacy.
